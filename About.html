About SINA Corporation

SINA Corporation for General Trading is the exclusive authorized agent in Yemen for FARGO-HID.

The company provides complete solutions for automatic card issuance, including plastic cards, ID cards, card printers, printer ribbons, cleaning kits, spare parts, and technical support.

FARGO card printers are known for their high quality, ease of use, and professional printing results.

SINA Corporation also provides after-sales services, including printer maintenance, spare parts, and customer technical support.
